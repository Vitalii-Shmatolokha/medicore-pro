Data Governance

HIPAA & GDPR Privacy Policy

Security Standard: AES-256 Encrypted At-Rest & In-Transit

1. Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

MediCore Pro handles Electronic Health Records (EHR), diagnostic summaries, and prescription requests in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Security Rule and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Patient records are strictly accessible by authorized care teams.

2. Video Stream Encryption & Media Recording

Peer-to-peer telemedicine consultations utilize WebRTC encryption with Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-time Transport Protocol (SRTP). In-call recordings are generated client-side and saved locally unless explicitly exported to patient EHR archives upon mutual clinical consent.

3. Access Logs & Audit Accountability

Every clinical record mutation, prescription issuance, and administrative modification generates an immutable audit timestamp recording the user ID and timestamp to guarantee clinical data provenance.
