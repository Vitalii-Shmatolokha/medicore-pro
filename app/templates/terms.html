Standard Clinical Service Terms

1. Clinical Scope & Telemedicine Limitations

MediCore Pro provides real-time audiovisual consultation, appointment scheduling, and electronic prescription workflow services for licensed medical practitioners and enrolled patients. Telemedicine services are strictly reserved for non-emergent medical assessments. In life-threatening emergencies, patients must immediately contact localized emergency dispatch (911/112).

2. Patient Responsibilities & Credential Integrity

Users are required to provide authentic, verifiable identification and maintain accurate medical history records. Account credentials must remain confidential. Practitioners agree to operate solely within their licensed jurisdictions and maintain active board certifications.

3. Prescription Orders & Medication Compliance

E-prescriptions generated through this portal represent clinical decisions by independent attending physicians. Prescriptions must be fulfilled by accredited pharmacies adhering to regional scheduled pharmaceutical regulations.
